Camera and geometry fidelity

The generated cameras do not match exactly. The models receive reference frames, not hard constraints for Blender position, rotation and focal length. Equal playback times do not guarantee equal viewpoints. Sign restoration through compositing and exact camera matching have not been demonstrated.

04 Combine a refined image and 3DGS for a compositing plate

Use Seedream to establish the desired appearance and 3DGS video to supply composition and motion. This pair improved materials and poster retention in our test, but faithful reproduction is not yet guaranteed. Treat appearance and camera, lettering and geometry preservation as separate tasks.

Seedance remains a candidate

Seedance with the Seedream reference improved sunny materials and poster retention. Small lettering and window grids changed, and an extra wordmark appeared, so it did not pass exact location preservation. Its image-plus-video cost was US$3.83 equivalent for about four seconds, versus US$0.93 for the three-second Aleph workflow. Different conditions prevent a definitive quality ranking.

Remaining camera and lighting limitations

Video reference does not lock the generated camera or geometry. Strict compositing requires checking every frame against the original-camera render. Full HD output also does not guarantee sharp lettering or window detail.

Establish sun direction, shadow softness and exposure, then match the reference image and added CG. Captured shadows remain baked into 3DGS colour; adding lights does not remove them. Free relighting and CG contact/shadow compositing were not tested here.

Generated result: Seedream + Aleph 2.0

The custom Seedream first frame was propagated through the original 3DGS clip with Aleph 2.0 Edit Studio. The original download is 1920×1080, 24fps and 72 frames. No AI upscale or colour correction was applied.

Daylight appearance and the large DMM poster carry through. Small lettering, poster faces and road details are still reconstructed. The orbit broadly follows the source, but exact camera and geometry matching have not been established. This is a generated background candidate, not an approved final compositing plate. Lettering and geometry repair and an actual CG contact/shadow test remain necessary for production use.
