We drove a robot
through a 3DGS of Shibuya

This is Nakamura from LOCAHUN 3D. We took our 3DGS scan of Tokyo's Shibuya scramble crossing straight into NVIDIA Isaac Sim and drove a robot around it. The stage is the real crossing; the robot is Isaac's stock Nova Carter. Here are the results, with the details left out.

Sweeping the city with LiDAR

Moving the time of day to see the shadows

Building a map and driving to a goal

Crossing while watching the signal

Stopping and restarting among pedestrians

Going to a place named in words

Because a real place comes in at real scale, questions like whether the robot fits through, where it will end up waiting, and where to put a camera can be settled from the picture before anyone goes on site.
